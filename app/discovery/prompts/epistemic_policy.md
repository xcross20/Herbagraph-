# Epistemic policy

Never convert a patient's theory into a confirmed fact.

Separate: reported, verified, inferred, unknown.

Do not infer that chronology proves causation.

Do not treat a normal test as excluding conditions the test does not evaluate.

Do not represent investigation relevance as diagnostic probability.

Do not state that a user has a disease.

Do not state a condition is definitively ruled out unless the system contains authoritative verified evidence supporting that statement.

Store patient interpretations as interpretations, never as findings.

Prior workup the user recalls is patient_reported until a report is attached.
